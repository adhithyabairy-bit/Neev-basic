Step-by-Step Architecture

The 9-Step Pedagogical Workflow

Neev replaces black-box grading with an accountable, teacher-in-the-loop diagnostic pipeline built for real classroom examinations.

Step 1 — Class & Syllabus Configuration

Select Grade (6 to 10), Academic Term, and Subject. Map lessons or upload custom textbook chapters. Neev loads the prerequisite dependency graph automatically.

Step 2 — Upload Answer Sheets

Upload student handwritten answer sheet images or PDFs alongside optional Question Paper and Marking Scheme scans. Neev performs instant staging and validation.

Step 3 — Vision Extraction & Preview

Google Gemini Multimodal Vision transcribes student manuscripts into structured markdown answers. Raw extracted text is preserved for full auditability.

Step 4 — Side-by-Side Teacher Verification

The critical safeguard. Teachers review transcribed text directly alongside original scans, correcting OCR errors and confirming awarded marks before any AI diagnostic runs.

Step 5 — Grounded AI Diagnostic Analysis

Vector RAG retrieves relevant textbook passages from MongoDB Atlas. AI evaluates student answers against factual curriculum rubrics, documenting misconceptions with citations.

Step 6 — Root Prerequisite Gap Synthesis

Identifies root causes tracing back to earlier grades (e.g. Class 7 integers causing Class 8 algebraic errors). Categorizes gaps into High, Medium, and Low severity with evidence.

Step 7 — Learning Remediation Roadmap

Generates 3 actionable phases: Immediate Unblocking, Foundational Reinforcement, and Grade-Level Mastery with target completion weeks and concrete textbook references.

Step 8 — Diagnostic Gap-Based Test

Auto-generates customized re-test questions specifically targeting diagnosed misconceptions, providing teachers ready-to-print assessment items.

Step 9 — Export Follow-Up Reports

Produces 5 professional PDF documents with clean typography, tables, page numbering, and bilingual English-Telugu / English-Hindi support for parents and school administration.
