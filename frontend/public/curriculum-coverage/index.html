Complete Academic Curriculum Coverage

Neev supports national and state standards while enabling individual teachers to upload private institution-specific syllabi.

Mathematics (Grades 6-10)

Integers, Rational Numbers, Linear Equations, Algebraic Identities, Triangles, Quadrilaterals, Mensuration, Coordinate Geometry, Trigonometry, and Probability.

Science (Grades 6-10)

Cell Biology, Microorganisms, Force & Pressure, Light & Optics, Chemical Reactions, Acids & Bases, Periodic Classification, Electricity, and Human Physiology.

Social Science (Grades 6-10)

Our Pasts, Resources & Development, Democratic Politics, Economic Development, Climate & Drainage, and Indian Constitution.
