Our Mission

Strengthening the Foundation of Learning

'Neev' means foundation. We believe no student falls behind intentionally — they fall behind when an unaddressed foundational gap in an earlier grade compounds into fear of learning.

Empowering Teachers, Not Replacing Them

We believe the teacher-student relationship is irreplaceable. Our technology takes on administrative transcription so teachers can focus on targeted pedagogical intervention.

Pedagogical Accountability

We do not generate ungrounded generative predictions. Every diagnostic insight is grounded in certified curriculum documents and verified by human educators.
