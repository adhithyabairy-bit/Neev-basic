Student Data Protection

Privacy Policy & Data Security

We maintain the highest standards of data security to protect schools, educators, and children's academic records.

1. Student Data Confidentiality

Student answer sheets, scores, and diagnostic reports belong solely to the school and teacher. We never sell student data or train third-party foundation models on student submissions.

2. Strict Multi-Tenant Isolation

Every assessment, student roster, and custom curriculum document is encrypted and partitioned at the database layer. No school can view another school's data.

3. Compliance with Indian DPDP Act

Neev adheres to the principles of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, ensuring lawful processing, explicit purpose limitation, and the right to erasure for educational institutions.
