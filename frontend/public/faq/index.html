Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how Neev empowers teachers and diagnoses learning gaps.

How does Neev handle messy student handwriting?

Neev utilizes Gemini Multimodal Vision optimized for student manuscripts, mathematical notations, and diagrams. Our side-by-side Step 4 interface lets teachers make instant edits with 100% fidelity.

Can the AI grade incorrectly without the teacher noticing?

No. Teacher authority is paramount. Neev enforces a strict teacher-in-the-loop verification step. The AI acts as an assistant providing textbook-cited hypotheses, not an unverified grader.

Is student assessment data private and secure?

Yes. All student answer sheets and teacher syllabus documents are scoped to authenticated teacher accounts with strict multi-tenant isolation, encrypted storage, and rate limiting.

What reports can I export for parents and administration?

Neev produces 5 PDF reports including Student Diagnostic Summaries, Parent Guidance sheets with regional language phonetics (Telugu & Hindi), Teacher Worksheets, and Class Gap Overviews.
